// 02 · sim lab

How I teach robots

Real robots are slow, expensive and break when they fall over. So I copy the world into simulation, let the robot practise there thousands of times in parallel, and bring back only what works.

01
Capture reality

Measure the real robot and cell: geometry, masses, cameras, latencies.

gadget: Copy Robot
02
Build the twin

Rebuild it in Isaac Sim with matched physics and sensors — a digital twin I can trust.

gadget: Small Light, in reverse
03
Train in parallel

Thousands of environments at once in Isaac Lab — RL with PPO, imitation with ACT & Diffusion Policy.

gadget: Time Room
04
Ship to real

Domain randomisation, validation, then the policy runs on the actual robot. Sim-to-real.

gadget: Anywhere Door

What I do
  • Simulation & digital twins — Isaac Sim scenes that behave like the real cell, for testing and synthetic data.
  • Robot learning — RL (PPO) and imitation learning (ACT, Diffusion Policy) in massively parallel Isaac Lab envs.
  • Motion & control — RMPFlow, MoveIt 2 and collision-aware planning that's smooth on real arms.
  • The whole stack — from soldering a board and EtherCAT drivers to a full ROS 2 deployment.
// 03 · pulled from the pocket

Gadgets I've built

Tap any card to watch it run.

Isaac Sim Policy — Inference

Manipulation policies (ACT, Diffusion Policy) trained entirely inside NVIDIA Isaac Lab with parallel, manager-based environments — then run for real. Part of the HIR teleoperation system.

#IsaacLab#ACT#DiffusionPolicy#Sim2Real

Real2Sim — Digital Twin

A digital twin of our robot cloned into Isaac Sim, so we can generate synthetic training data instead of paying for expensive real-world collection.

#IsaacSim#DigitalTwin#SyntheticData

RL with PPO — Isaac Lab

Teaching a custom-designed dexterous robotic hand in-hand manipulation with PPO in Isaac Lab. Currently at a 64% success rate and climbing.

#IsaacLab#PPO#RL#DexterousHand

RMPFlow + Collision Avoidance

RMPFlow integrated with Isaac Sim and MoveIt 2 for smooth, reactive, collision-aware arm motion in cluttered scenes.

#RMPFlow#IsaacSim#MoveIt2

Alice — AI Robo Bartender

A robot agent that chats with you through OpenAI LLMs wired into ROS, then mixes your drink. Runs on GCP with Pub/Sub for robot-state monitoring, MoveIt 2 + OctoMap for collision-aware pouring, and a FastAPI bridge to the outside world.

#ROS2#OpenAI#MoveIt2#GCP#FastAPI

ScrewQC — Robotic Inspection

A vision-assisted robot that inspects screws to 0.01 mm precision. A Doosan A0509 over MODBUS, an OpenCV eigenvector trick to localise each screw, YOLOv8 for detection, and Gazebo + MoveIt for motion planning.

#OpenCV#YOLOv8#Doosan#MODBUS#Gazebo

Canis — Robot Dog

A quadruped robot (canis = Latin for dog) with its own legged-locomotion stack. Equal parts engineering and weekend obsession.

#Quadruped#Locomotion#ROS2

Vision-Based TeleOperation

A motion-capture algorithm that lets plain hand motion drive a robot — no gloves, no extra hardware, just a camera. Core project of the HIR team.

#ComputerVision#TeleOp#MotionCapture#ROS2

Teleoperative Glove

A sensor-packed glove that captures the operator's hand pose and streams it to the robot, making teleoperation as natural as putting on a glove.

#Embedded#Sensors#TeleOp

Custom Robotic Gripper

A custom gripper with its own ROS packages for simulation in Gazebo Classic and a MoveIt Setup Assistant config for planning and control.

#ROS2#Gazebo#MoveIt#CAD

Human Safety Velocity Control

A safety layer for shared workspaces: when a person steps into the robot's space, it smoothly slows down or stops.

#Safety#ControlSystems#ROS2

Robo-Artist

A robot arm that turns images into stroke plans and draws them — where motion planning meets a bit of creative expression.

#RobotArm#PathPlanning#CreativeAI
